How the value is worked out — model radar-2

implied = TVL × comparable × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Comparable. The median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in this snapshot (ratios outside 0–20 dropped). This snapshot's median is ….
  • Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%..+100%, weighted 0.8: 1 + 0.8 × growth / 100. A 7-day move is noisy, so it moves value by at most −40% / +80%.
  • Maturity. 0.55 + 0.45 × min(age, 1460 days) / 1460, where age is snapshot date minus the day DefiLlama began tracking the protocol. Unknown age counts as 365 days. Time with no token is the patience premium, worth at most +45% over four years.
  • Breadth. 1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 5): a protocol live on several chains has stickier TVL. A tie-breaker, never the story.

What the board leaves out, and why: exchanges, bridges, staking products and versioned pools (“Name V2”) are products of a token that already trades, not protocols still waiting on a launch of their own. “Records no token” means DefiLlama's record carries no token for the protocol — it is not proof that none exists. A farm plan positions a wallet; it does not guarantee a token, an airdrop, or any payout. Implied values are this entry's own claim.
